\item Core contributor to
  \href{https://github.com/AutoFixture/AutoFixture}{AutoFixture} (2011—2016),

  \href{https://blog.ploeh.dk/2013/03/01/AutoFixture3/}{AutoFixture 3} and

  Passes all dieharder tests and used in Hedgehog for replay and
  shrinking—critical components for reproducible failure discovery.
